How to add invite link in bio?
Adding your unique referral link to your Telegram bio helps you earn extra bonus WAVES automatically when your friends join through it!
  • 1
    Go to your Telegram profile settings and tap on your Bio.
  • 2
    Copy your personal invite link from the Refer section of XriAura.
  • 3
    Paste the link into your bio and save changes. Done!
